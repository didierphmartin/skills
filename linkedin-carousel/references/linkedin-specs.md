# LinkedIn Carousel Specifications

This document contains technical specifications and best practices for LinkedIn carousel posts.

## Technical Requirements

### Image Format
- **Format**: PDF (LinkedIn converts each page to an image)
- **Alternative**: Individual PNG/JPG images (but PDF is preferred for ease of upload)
- **Aspect Ratio**: 1:1 (square) is most common, but 1.91:1 (landscape) also works
- **Recommended Size**: 1080x1080px for square format
- **File Size**: Maximum 100MB for PDF, but keep under 10MB for fast loading
- **Page Limit**: 2-20 slides (LinkedIn's limit)

### Color Space
- **RGB** color space (not CMYK)
- Ensure sufficient contrast for mobile readability
- Test colors on both light and dark mode if possible

## Design Best Practices

### Typography
- **Minimum font size**: 24pt for body text (readable on mobile)
- **Title font size**: 40-60pt
- **Font choices**: Sans-serif fonts (Arial, Helvetica, Roboto) are most readable on screens
- **Line spacing**: 1.3-1.5x for comfortable reading
- **Text alignment**: Left-aligned for body text, centered for titles

### Layout
- **Margins**: Minimum 40-60px on all sides
- **White space**: Use generously to avoid cluttered slides
- **Hierarchy**: Clear visual hierarchy with title, body, and optional footer
- **Consistency**: Maintain consistent layout across all slides

### Content Structure
- **Slide 1**: Hook/title that grabs attention
- **Slides 2-N-1**: Core content (one key idea per slide)
- **Slide N**: Call-to-action or summary
- **Optimal length**: 5-10 slides (engagement drops after 10)

### Color Guidelines
- **Professional palette**: Blues, grays, navy (LinkedIn's brand colors)
- **Accent colors**: Use sparingly for emphasis
- **Contrast ratio**: Minimum 4.5:1 for text (WCAG AA standard)
- **Background**: Light backgrounds with dark text are most readable

## Engagement Best Practices

### Content Tips
1. **Start strong**: First slide should hook the reader immediately
2. **One idea per slide**: Don't overload with information
3. **Use numbers**: Lists and numbered items perform well
4. **Tell a story**: Create narrative flow across slides
5. **End with CTA**: Clear next step or question for engagement

### Text Guidelines
- **Brevity**: 50-100 words per slide maximum
- **Bullet points**: Use for easy scanning
- **Bold key phrases**: Highlight important takeaways
- **Questions**: Engage readers with thought-provoking questions
- **Avoid jargon**: Use clear, accessible language

### Visual Elements
- **Icons**: Simple icons can enhance comprehension
- **Charts/graphs**: Keep simple and clearly labeled
- **Photos**: Use high-quality images if including photos
- **Branding**: Subtle logo or watermark (don't overpower content)

## LinkedIn Algorithm Considerations

### Engagement Factors
- **Dwell time**: More slides = more time spent = better reach
- **Swipe rate**: Engaging content encourages swipes
- **Comments**: Carousels generate more comments than single images
- **Saves**: High-value content gets saved and reshared

### Posting Tips
- **Post caption**: Write a compelling caption (1300 character limit)
- **Hashtags**: 3-5 relevant hashtags
- **Timing**: Post when your audience is most active
- **First comment**: Add a detailed first comment with additional context
- **Tag relevant people**: Mention contributors or relevant connections

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Too much text**: Slides that are walls of text get skipped
2. **Inconsistent design**: Jarring style changes between slides
3. **Poor contrast**: Light text on light backgrounds
4. **Tiny fonts**: Unreadable on mobile devices
5. **No clear flow**: Random slides without narrative connection
6. **Weak opening**: Boring first slide loses viewers immediately
7. **No CTA**: Missing opportunity to drive action
8. **Too many slides**: Engagement drops significantly after 10 slides

## Accessibility Considerations

- **Alt text**: LinkedIn doesn't support alt text for carousel images, so ensure text is readable in the image itself
- **Color blindness**: Don't rely solely on color to convey information
- **High contrast**: Ensure text is readable for users with vision impairments
- **Simple language**: Make content accessible to non-native speakers

## Performance Benchmarks

### Average Engagement Rates
- **Carousel posts**: 1.5-3x more engagement than single image posts
- **Optimal length**: 6-8 slides typically perform best
- **Completion rate**: 40-60% of viewers see the last slide (if content is engaging)

### Success Metrics to Track
- **Impressions**: How many people saw the post
- **Engagement rate**: Likes, comments, shares, and saves
- **Click-through rate**: If linking to external content
- **Follower growth**: New followers from the post
- **Slide-through rate**: What percentage viewed all slides
